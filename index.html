01 - Why Third Places?

Agora - Third Places

When people talk about what makes cities feel vibrant, they rarely mention infrastructure or technology.

They talk about their favorite café, the bookstore they visit every weekend, or the park where they unexpectedly met someone new.

These spaces, often called third places, exist outside of home and work and play a critical role in building community, creativity, and belonging.

Yet despite growing interest in meaningful social connection, these spaces are becoming increasingly difficult to find, sustain, and incorporate into everyday life.

Our team set out to explore why.

"What if the most important places in your life aren't where you live or work, but the spaces in between?"

01 - Why Klinos?

Klinos

Healthcare isn't broken from a lack of services. The doctors, specialists, and insurance are all there. What's missing is coordination between them.

Patients are often left managing referrals, transferring records, scheduling appointments, and navigating disconnected providers on their own. Even individuals with premium insurance and access to top healthcare systems experience delays, duplicated tests, and fragmented care journeys.

Klinos was founded to solve this problem by creating a vertically integrated healthcare platform that combines longevity medicine, advanced prevention, and on-demand care under one coordinated experience.

02 - The Challenge

During discovery, one challenge became immediately apparent: users didn't understand what made Klinos different.

Most healthcare companies focus on individual services, while Klinos was offering something much larger — a coordinated healthcare ecosystem designed around prevention and long-term wellbeing.

How might we communicate a complex healthcare model in a way that feels intuitive, trustworthy, and valuable?

01 - It's 9pm and I'm Retyping Myself

Relay — carrying context across your AI tools

I'm explaining the same project background to Claude that I already explained to ChatGPT twenty minutes ago. Same context, same three paragraphs, different tab. I do this almost daily — one tool for writing, another for code, another for a quick fact-check — and every switch costs me five minutes of re-explaining myself before I can actually get to the task.

I wanted to know if this was just my workflow being messy, or something most people juggling AI tools quietly deal with.

🎨
My Role
UX/UI Designer & Researcher
⏱️
Timeline
3 Weeks
🛠️
Tools
Figma · Maze · Google Forms
📦
Type
Concept Project
02 - What I Found

I ran a 6-day diary study with 4 participants screened for one thing: using 2+ AI tools at least 3-4 times a week. I paired it with a short survey posted in two AI-enthusiast communities to see if the pattern held beyond four people.

It wasn't just me:

  • Context loss, not capability, was the top complaint — the pain showed up after switching tools, re-establishing background the new one didn't have
  • Copy-paste was the default handoff, and it was lossy — people pasted a summary, rarely the full reasoning
  • Tool choice was task-based, not loyalty-based — everyone had a mental map of "which tool for what," with no way to carry anything between stops
  • Subscription fatigue was real, but ranked behind context loss as a daily frustration
  • The survey backed up the diary — a majority of respondents said they regularly re-explain themselves to a second tool in the same day
03 - Who This Is Really For
J
Jordan, Software Engineer
High comfort with every AI tool individually — the friction is entirely in the handoff between them.

"I always have the right answer somewhere — I just can't remember which tab it's in."

How might we let people carry context between AI tools, without forcing them onto just one?
04 - The Journey, Stage by Stage
01
Working in Tool A

Drafting or coding, focused.

02
Hits a Wall

Realizes another tool fits better.

03
Opens Tool B

"What did I already tell the other one?"

04
Re-explains

Pastes or retypes background, frustrated.

05
Returns to Tool A

Low trust that nothing got lost.

05 - Three Directions, One Decision
🧩
Browser Extension

A context clipboard. Fast, but invisible and easy to forget.

🔌
In-Tool Plugin

Cleanest integration, but needs buy-in from every tool vendor.

🗂️
Standalone Dashboard

Chosen — sits alongside tools people already use, no vendor cooperation required.

06 - Introducing Relay

A dashboard that tracks your active AI conversations and lets you send context to another tool deliberately — reviewed and editable — instead of pasting blind.

🗂️
Dashboard

Active threads, tagged by tool, with a freshness indicator.

📝
Thread Detail

Auto-summarized key facts, full transcript collapsed underneath.

↗️
Handoff Flow

Pick a destination, preview and edit what carries over, send.

⚙️
Settings

Connect tools, control exactly what Relay is allowed to capture.

07 - Key Design Decisions
🏷️
Tool-First Cards

Threads lead with the originating tool, not the task — that's how people actually navigate.

✏️
Editable Handoff

Copy-paste failed because nobody reviewed what carried over. Relay makes review the default.

🔒
Explicit Capture Control

No "everything, always" default — added directly from a usability finding below.

08 - Next Steps
This is a concept, not a shipped product. Four diary participants and a community survey are a starting signal, not proof — the next pass needs a real two-tool integration and a bigger, more varied sample before any of this gets treated as settled.
