01 - Why Third Places?

Agora - Third Places

When people talk about what makes cities feel vibrant, they rarely mention infrastructure or technology.

They talk about their favorite café, the bookstore they visit every weekend, or the park where they unexpectedly met someone new.

These spaces, often called third places, exist outside of home and work and play a critical role in building community, creativity, and belonging.

Yet despite growing interest in meaningful social connection, these spaces are becoming increasingly difficult to find, sustain, and incorporate into everyday life.

Our team set out to explore why.

"What if the most important places in your life aren't where you live or work, but the spaces in between?"

04 - Defining the Challenge
How might we make third spaces discoverable while incentivizing people to return regularly as part of their routine?

American society is increasingly seeking the European way of living through third spaces for community and enjoyment. However, many people struggle to discover these spaces, and current third spaces are often not financially sustainable for business owners, reducing overall supply.

05 - Introducing Agora

Named after the ancient Greek word for "gathering place," Agora is a dual digital + physical platform.

📱
Digital Platform

Mobile app with curated listings, mood-based filtering, community reviews, and a roulette feature for serendipitous discovery.

🛍️
Physical Touchpoints

Branded cups, "linger longer" tote bags, and connection cards placed in partner spaces to spark real-world conversation.

🏪
Business Model

Affiliate marketing, featured placements, and B2B connections to magazines and event coordinators for sustainability.

🎲

A serendipity mode that suggests a spontaneous third place outing, lowering decision friction and building habits.

07 - The Physical Solution

The team believed the app alone wouldn't shift behavior. Physical touchpoints were prototyped to organically foster connection inside partner spaces: tote bags reading "linger longer," branded coffee cups with community messaging, and connection cards with fields for name, favorite spot, and contact info.

01 - Why Klinos?

Klinos

Healthcare isn't broken from a lack of services. The doctors, specialists, and insurance are all there. What's missing is coordination between them.

Patients are often left managing referrals, transferring records, scheduling appointments, and navigating disconnected providers on their own. Even individuals with premium insurance and access to top healthcare systems experience delays, duplicated tests, and fragmented care journeys.

Klinos was founded to solve this problem by creating a vertically integrated healthcare platform that combines longevity medicine, advanced prevention, and on-demand care under one coordinated experience.

02 - The Challenge

During discovery, one challenge became immediately apparent: users didn't understand what made Klinos different.

Most healthcare companies focus on individual services, while Klinos was offering something much larger — a coordinated healthcare ecosystem designed around prevention and long-term wellbeing.

How might we communicate a complex healthcare model in a way that feels intuitive, trustworthy, and valuable?
03 - Understanding the User

Through stakeholder discussions, market research, and competitor analysis, I identified a recurring pattern. People weren't frustrated by a lack of healthcare options, they were frustrated by the effort required to coordinate everything themselves.

People don't need more healthcare services. They need a better way to navigate them.
04 - Designing the Ecosystem
🌐
Website Experience

The first introduction to Klinos, focused on educating, building trust, and communicating the value of coordinated healthcare.

📱
Mobile App Experience

A centralized hub where members access health information, manage appointments, and stay connected with their care team.

05 - Website Design Goals

Transforming a complex healthcare model into a story users can easily understand and act on.

  • Simplifying complex healthcare offerings into clear service pathways
  • Building trust through educational content and transparent messaging
  • Creating intuitive navigation across multiple healthcare programs
  • Guiding users toward consultations and membership enrollment

06 - Mobile App Experience

While the website introduces users to Klinos, the mobile app supports them throughout their ongoing healthcare journey.

📊

💬

07 - Key Design Decisions
🎯
Outcomes Over Services

Structured content around user goals and health outcomes.

🧩
Simplifying Information

Used progressive disclosure, clear hierarchy, and simplified language.

🤝
Trust Through Transparency

Educational content helped users feel confident in their decisions.

📐
Designing for Scale

A reusable design system supports consistency and future growth.

08 - Outcome & Impact
  • Established a unified digital ecosystem across web and mobile touchpoints
  • Improved understanding of healthcare services through simplified navigation
  • Built a scalable design system supporting future products and platform growth
  • Strengthened trust through clear, educational design

01 - Why Best Buy Connect?

Best Buy Connect

Redesigning Internal Communication by Listening to What Employees Actually Needed

Large organizations rely on internal communication tools to keep employees informed, connected, and engaged.

At Best Buy, the existing employee platform served as a central hub for company information, but adoption remained low. Employees were increasingly turning to external tools and informal channels to communicate, share knowledge, and build community.

02 - The Challenge

The real challenge was not communication during work — it was communication outside of work. Employees wanted a space where they could connect with peers, share experiences, and build relationships beyond their immediate teams.

How might we create a platform that fosters community and connection across the entire Best Buy employee network?
03 - Research & Discovery
🔍

Benchmarked Slack, Microsoft Teams, Meta Workplace, and Yammer.

🎤

📋
CPS Framework

04 - Reframing the Experience
People didn't need another channel for announcements. They needed a reason to open the app between shifts.
05 - The Design System
06 - The Solution

Community Feed

A central homepage surfaces company updates, team highlights, and ongoing conversations.

👥
Groups & Communities

📢

🔒

  • Designed a groups system that lets employees connect across departments and locations
  • Separated official communication from community discussion to reduce noise
  • Introduced privacy controls that respect varying comfort levels with visibility

01 - The Forgotten Footage Problem

HeroFlow for Lemonlight Studios

Video production agencies generate enormous amounts of content. For Lemonlight Studios, years of client work had resulted in thousands of hours of footage spread across campaigns. While this archive held immense value, most of it remained effectively inaccessible.

Teams often knew the footage existed. They just couldn't find it. What should have been a competitive advantage had become a bottleneck.

🎨
My Role
Product Designer & Researcher
👥
Team
5-Person Cross-Disciplinary
⏱️
Timeline
5-Day Product Sprint
📦
Deliverables
Product Strategy · Prototype · Pitch Deck
02 - The Challenge

"I know we already shot something like this, but finding it takes longer than creating something new."

How might we help creative teams instantly discover, understand, and reuse existing footage through AI?
03 - Understanding the Workflow
  • Editors became the source of truth for where footage lived, creating bottlenecks that didn't scale
  • Metadata quality varied significantly between projects, making traditional search unreliable
  • Even when footage was located, users had to watch clips individually to judge relevance
04 - Reframing the Opportunity
Creative teams didn't just want to find footage. They wanted to immediately use it.
05 - Introducing HeroFlow

An AI-powered video intelligence platform that helps teams discover, understand, and repurpose content through natural language.

🔍
Semantic Search

Users search naturally and the platform surfaces relevant clips without manual tags.

📋
HeroFlow

AI-generated insights for every clip — scene description, emotional tone, subjects, and brand relevance.

✂️
Prompt-Based Editing

From a creative brief, users generate a draft edit assembled from relevant footage.

06 - End-to-End User Journey
01
Search

"Find lifestyle footage featuring families outdoors."

02
Discover

Semantic Search surfaces relevant clips across thousands of hours.

03
Understand

HeroFlow surfaces metadata and contextual insights per clip.

04
Create

User selects footage and generates a new edit through a prompt.

05
Ship

A reusable draft asset is ready within minutes, not hours.

07 - Key Design Decisions
✅
Editable Metadata

Users can correct AI-generated tags, keeping the system accurate over time.

📊
Confidence Indicators

Visibility into how certain the system is about its recommendations.

🔎
Search-First

Advanced functionality stays secondary, keeping the platform approachable.

⚖️
AI + Control

These decisions balanced automation with user control throughout.

08 - Impact
⏱️
6 hrs → 10 min

Target reduction in footage retrieval time per campaign.

📈
DAU / WAU

Daily and weekly active users as primary retention signals.

⭐
NPS Score

Net Promoter Score from agency and brand teams after onboarding.

♻️
Reuse Rate

Increase in repurposed video assets vs. newly produced content.

09 - What I Learned
"The most important challenge wasn't building AI features, it was designing trust around them."
